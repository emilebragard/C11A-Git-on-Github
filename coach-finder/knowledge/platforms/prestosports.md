# PrestoSports (most NJCAA sites, many NAIA/D2/D3)

- **Spot it:** URLs ending `/landing/index`, or "prestosports" in the HTML.
- **Coaches page:** `/sports/<presto_code>/coaches/index`, or with a season folder: `/sports/mbkb/2025-26/coaches`. Codes are in `knowledge/sports.csv`.
- **Staff directory:** `/information/directory/index` (sometimes `/athletics/directory/index`). Lists emails, and is often the best page.
- **Blocking:** PrestoSports' firewall **blocks cloud servers and often scripts** (403 "Request blocked"). This is the main reason the Chrome pass exists. In Chrome, pages load normally. Don't try to get around the block in scripts.
- **Web search** finds PrestoSports staff directory pages well, since search engines have them indexed. That's the fallback if Chrome isn't available.
