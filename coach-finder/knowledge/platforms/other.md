# Other platforms and plain college websites

- **WMT** (`wmt.digital` in the HTML): similar layout to Sidearm. Try `/sports/<sidearm_slug>/coaches` and `/staff-directory`.
- **Plain college sites** (athletics as a section of the college site, e.g. `school.edu/athletics/mens-basketball/`): coaches are often listed on the team page itself. Follow homepage links containing the sport name, then "coach" or "staff".
- **WordPress / Drupal:** no standard paths. Follow links, or use Chrome.
- **College main-site staff directories** (`/directory`, `/employee-directory`): great for email formats even when they don't list coaches (`scripts/college_dirs.py`).
- **Emails hidden** behind "contact" buttons or forms: record the name only. The email format step or search usually fills it in. Never submit forms.
